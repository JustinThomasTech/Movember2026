Initiative Disclaimer & T's & C's

This internal Mo-Grow contest is an independent workplace initiative designed to foster team connection and raise awareness for men's physical and mental health. It is not run in official collaboration with the formal Movember brand or global campaign. Should anyone wish to financially contribute to the initiative, you are highly encouraged to do so of your own accord directly via the official Movember website (za.movember.com).

Privacy & Consent: By taking part, registering an account, and creating your profile, you explicitly consent to your uploaded photos and captions being hosted on this web portal to be viewed and voted on by the business.
