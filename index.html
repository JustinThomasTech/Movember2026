Initiative Disclaimer & T's & C's

This internal Mo-Grow contest is an independent workplace initiative designed to foster team connection and raise awareness for men's physical and mental health. It is not run in official collaboration with the formal Movember brand or global campaign. Should anyone wish to financially contribute to the initiative, you are highly encouraged to do so of your own accord directly via the official Movember website (za.movember.com)[cite: 12].
