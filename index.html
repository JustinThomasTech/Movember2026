Rule 01 The Real Talk →

1. Clean Shave Baseline

All registered Mo-Bros must begin with a clean-shaven selfie on November 1.

Unfiltered Rule #1

The Babyface Protocol

No sneaky 3-day head starts allowed! On November 1st, your face must be as bare as a fresh sheet of paper. We need photographic evidence that you started from ground zero. Bid a temporary farewell to your emotional support stubble—it’s time to face the breeze.

Rule 02 The Real Talk →

2. The Moustache Only

No goatees or full beards. Only authentic upper-lip growth is evaluated.

Unfiltered Rule #2

Lip Island Only

This is a strictly solo expedition for your upper lip. That means no defensive goatees, no chinstraps, and no masquerading a five o'clock shadow as "texture." If the whiskers aren't confined strictly to the space between your nose and lip, you will be disqualified by popular outrage.

Rule 03 The Real Talk →

3. Company-Wide Vote

Prizes awarded at month-end based on growth, creativity, and participation.

Unfiltered Rule #3

Democracy, Banter & Bribery

Forget scientific calipers—this is an office popularity contest where pure charisma beats genetics every time. Flaunt your lip foliage on every Teams call, lobby ruthlessly in the kitchen, and remember: subtly trading barista coffee for final votes is practically an office tradition.
