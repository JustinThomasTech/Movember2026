1. Clean Shave Baseline

Unfiltered Rule #1

The Babyface Protocol

No sneaky head starts! On November 1st, your face must be as bare as a fresh sheet of paper. We need photographic evidence that you started from zero. Bid a temporary farewell to your emotional support stubble.

2. The Moustache Only

No goatees or full beards. Only authentic upper-lip growth is evaluated.

Unfiltered Rule #2

Lip Island Only

This is a strictly solo expedition for your upper lip. That means no defensive goatees, no chinstraps, and no five o'clock shadow. If it touches the chin, you are out.

Initiative Disclaimer & T's & C's

This Mo-Grow contest is an independent workplace initiative designed to foster team connection and raise awareness for men's physical and mental health. It is not run in official collaboration with the formal Movember brand or global campaign. Should anyone wish to financially contribute, you are highly encouraged to do so via the official Movember website (za.movember.com).
